# Main story

Labels are explained in [README.md](README.md#status-labels). Everything here
is **[Proposed]** except where marked; the record rules it builds on are
**[Implemented]** and described in PLAN.md, "Records in the wasteland".

> I set out to keep my shelter alive. I learned who lived here, followed what
> they left behind, and discovered that the people preying on this valley are
> still here. Now I have to decide what I will risk, and who I will stand
> with.

## What's built and what isn't

**[Implemented]** One slice: eight records about Ruth Lane and Toby Wren at
the Abandoned Supermarket and the Dry Reservoir, found one per trip, with
leads, an optional "What to look for" choice and a private journal. It
delivers an ordinary-life detail, an early connection (Kerry), the war
entering daily life (limits, ESD-31, the bus notice), a choice with a cost
(the 11 pallets), a partial answer (Ruth went north; the Wrens were ticked
onto Bus 2 and 46 of 48 arrived), and open questions (Kell Bridge, the dogs).
Catalogue: [fragments.md](fragments.md#implemented-records).

**[Proposed]** Everything else on this page: the five-stage arc, twelve more
records, the Ash Hounds' part, choices, endings and the continuity model.

## The five stages

**A. A home.**
- **Why you go out**: your purifier needs parts.
- **What you find**: the serial plate on your purifier stack
  (`unit-plate`); a drop-off board at the Workshop (`mags-dropboard`).
- **What it supports**: your Unit has a service history; someone signing
  M.H. still repairs Units.
- **Understanding**: your home was someone else's, and someone kept it
  working.
- **Next lead**: who is M.H.?
- **Choice**: leave payment in the tin for a repair (trading with someone
  you never see; later raises Mags's trust).
- **If you go elsewhere**: nothing is lost.

**B. People.**
- **Why**: curiosity; the eight implemented records give names.
- **What you find**: the camp at the Nest entrance, bowls with the old
  dogs' names (`chime-camp`).
- **What it supports**: someone lives near the dogs and feeds them.
- **Understanding**: the names from the drawings aren't only history.
- **Next leads**: Day 140's "old works main" points to the gallery; the camp
  points to whoever chalks warnings.
- **If you go elsewhere**: nothing is lost.

**C. Deliberate violence.**
- **Why**: the gallery lead.
- **What you find**: Dev's toolbag (`dev-toolbag`); the chained valve and
  Toby's chalk (`chained-valve`); the tag on the Workshop door
  (`tagged-door`); later the Ridge Road graves (`convoy-graves`).
- **What it supports**: people did this. Dogs don't chain valves or tag
  doors; the graves say "unarmed".
- **Understanding**: the valley's danger isn't only fallout and animals.
- **Next lead**: where the yellow tags come from, toward the Weighbridge.
- **Choice**: whether to tell other shelters now, through shelter talk.
- **If you wait**: the tags stay; nothing escalates on its own.

**D. A threat now.**
- **Why**: the tags, and chalked "Thursday" warnings (`chalk-warning`).
- **What you find**: the Weighbridge's ledger (`wade-ledger`), reachable
  while the Hounds are out on a run.
- **What it supports**: a planned winter run on the east side; Mags is on
  it, and so is the player's Unit, "new occupant, assess".
- **Understanding**: this is about you and your neighbours now.
- **Next leads**: protect, warn, open the bypass, confront.
- **If you wait**: Thursday runs keep happening. No countdown, no deadline.

**E. Responding.** See "Choices". Every route is open to a solo player; none
makes the player a soldier, an avenger, or someone with a past they didn't
choose.

## Choices

Each is worth something on its own. None requires treating the Ash Hounds'
murders as justified.

- **Warn**: share the schedule and ledger with other shelters through talk.
  Cost: you reveal what you know; a story consequence could tag your hatch.
- **Protect Mags**: warn her through the drop-off board, or move her stores.
  Cost: time away with your own shelter open.
- **Open the bypass** [Canon in outline, 2026-10-10]: the chained main valve
  stays chained; the way round it is the old scour line Dev died rather than
  show them ([world-bible.md](world-bible.md#the-bypass)). It is slow,
  quiet work over several trips, a quarter turn at a time, under the
  Ash Hounds' guard hut. It needs Toby, who knows the way, and Mags's or Kell
  Bridge's re-packing for the first water. Done wrong, it fouls Kell
  Bridge's main for weeks. Done right, Kell Bridge has water Wade doesn't
  control: about a third of what the main carried, enough to drink, cook
  and wash. His leverage breaks, provided enough people stand ready to
  keep the scour line open once he knows where it is [details Proposed].
- **Help Toby** [first part Canon, 2026-10-10; being implemented as records]:
  restore contact between Toby and his mother. You leave word for T in the
  Workshop's forwarding tin; he answers and asks for his post (Kerry's
  replies, held by Ruth at Kell Bridge), but doesn't want Ruth told where he
  is. Word goes north with the Kell Bridge walkers who pay Mags in chits;
  Ruth sends the letters south to the payment tin; Toby collects them and
  chalks his thanks at the bus shelter. Kell Bridge is still not a
  destination: everything happens through the Workshop's tins. Later, if
  approved: help him reach Ruth, or leave him to his own plan. He decides;
  you can help.
- **Recover and share out**: take stolen stores from the Weighbridge, then
  decide who gets them, an echo of Ruth's 11 pallets.
- **Deal**: Lena's ledger for her safe passage; Jace as an informant. Both
  cost something real.
- **Confront**: shelters coordinate against the Weighbridge.
- **Carry the letter**: take Helen's letter to Ruth, or don't.

Current rules stay as they are: combat uses the existing equipment-loss
rules; any story raid on a shelter uses the existing raid-loss limits; no
shelter is ever destroyed. None of these choices is implemented.

## Endings

Reached by the community's combined actions (see "Solo and shared play").

1. **"The road reopens"** (mostly non-combat). The bypass is opened and Kell
   Bridge's drinking water returns without passing the chained valve. Shared schedules make ambushes fail; Lena defects
   and Jace walks away. Wade keeps the Weighbridge with fewer people: weaker,
   still dangerous. Toby reaches Ruth; the letter arrives if someone carried
   it. Travel is possible again, in groups.
2. **"The Weighbridge falls"**. Shelters act together; the captives and Jace
   open the gate. Wade is taken to Kell Bridge to answer for what he did, or
   dies there, as the community's actions decide. Stores recovered; who gets
   them is the next quarrel. It costs injuries and possibly a supporting
   character's life; survivors scatter into small bands, the seed of later
   stories.
3. **"Hold the valley"**. Shelters keep watch for each other and the east side
   becomes too costly to raid. The road stays shut and Wade turns north on
   Kell Bridge. Toby goes north alone along the scour drain and the old main. The valley is safe at
   someone else's cost, and that should sit uncomfortably.

Each answers what happened to the people followed, what happens to the
immediate threat, and what changes for travel.

## Solo and shared play

Three models were considered:

| Model | Gain | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Each player has their own Wade | simple | a shared world contradicts itself: you kill him, your neighbour still meets him |
| The first group to act changes the world for everyone | a real shared world | latecomers miss everything; needs a campaign system |
| **Shared history and threat, personal discovery, community thresholds** | one Wade; solo progress; cooperation counts | endings are slower and collective; needs a little shared state |

**Recommended [Proposed, Open]**: the third.
- **Shared by all**: the history (records are personal copies of shared
  facts) and the Ash Hounds' existence and current state.
- **Personal**: what you've found, leads you hold, whom you helped, whether
  you carried the letter.
- **Repeatable**: encounters with unnamed Ash Hound raiders, if a fight is
  ever approved; the Thursday runs. Never Wade or a named character.
- **Shared present-day events**: tags appearing on shelters; a story raid
  within existing limits.
- **Permanent change**: only at the community level. A shared count of
  pressure on the Ash Hounds rises with warnings shared, the bypass opened,
  stores recovered, defections. Crossing a threshold changes the world for
  everyone at once and records each player's part.

A solo player can finish the whole historical story and push the count alone;
ending 1 should be reachable through solo contributions over time. Nobody
needs another player online. None of this exists in the game; it needs
shared state the game doesn't have.

## First chapter after the slice: "The Old Works"

**[Proposed, Open]**. Six records at existing places: `unit-plate` (your
shelter), `mags-dropboard` and `tagged-door` (Workshop), `dev-toolbag` and
`chained-valve` (the Reservoir gallery, by a lead from `day-140` or
`radio-log`), `chime-camp` (Nest entrance).

The arc: a home with a history, a person still caring for things, a murder in
the water works, a name ("Mercer"), and a fresh tag on a door you know. It
ends with the Ash Hounds known, Toby probably alive, Mags probably alive, and
the Weighbridge the obvious next place.

Not in it: new destinations, Ash Hound fights, the shared count.

It would need records at the Workshop and the Nest, a way to inspect the
purifier at home, and one new lead. After crit 9 (2026-10-14).
