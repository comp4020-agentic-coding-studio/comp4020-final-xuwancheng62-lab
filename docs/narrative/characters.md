# Characters

Labels are explained in [README.md](README.md#status-labels).

**[Canon]** for all five: they are preserved as distinct, connected people;
their ages on the Ninth and now are fixed ([world-bible.md](world-bible.md#dates-and-ages));
what the eight implemented records say about them is fact in the world.

**[Canon]** since 2026-10-10: each person's fate (where they went after Day
140, their key later decision, how they ended up, where they are now), as
summarised under "Writer's truth". **[Proposed]** still applies to smaller
details marked as such below, and to anything a record would show that isn't
in the game yet.

Each person has: life before; what they wanted; their decisions and who paid
for them; a contradiction; a timeline; relationships; the writer's truth; and
**what a player can establish** from evidence. Evidence IDs refer to
[fragments.md](fragments.md).

## Ruth Lane

**Life before** [Canon in outline]: twenty years at FreshWay Calder, night
manager. Knows every regular. Her favourite enemy is Gary, who argues every
limit.

**Wanted**: to keep her street fed and her staff together.

**Decisions and costs**
- [Canon] On the Ninth she sent 11 of 22 pallets to Northfield under ESD-31
  and kept 11 for the 140 people in CS-4. CS-4, then the 23 at the pumping
  station, ate them. Northfield's 2,300, among them Kerry and Toby, went to one
  meal a day 11 pallets short.
- [Canon] She gave her own Bus 2 seat to the Patterson boy.
- [Canon, 2026-10-10] In Y+4, after Dev's death, she refused to pay the Ash
  Hounds for water from the chained valve, choosing rations at Kell Bridge
  over submission. Kell Bridge is divided about it.
- [Canon, 2026-10-10] She holds three sealed letters from Kerry Wren
  addressed to Toby "care of R. Lane, Kell Bridge exchange — hold for him".
  She doesn't open other people's post. She doesn't know Toby is back in
  Calder; since 18 May Y+4 she has feared he died with Dev.

**Contradiction**: "same for everyone", while Gary's kids get her share on
Sundays; calls the council useless, and moved the instant Helen phoned.

**Timeline**
- [Canon] Weeks before the Ninth: purchase limits (`ration-sign`).
- [Canon] The Ninth: ESD-31; seat given away; CS-4.
- [Canon] Day 15: 23 move to the pumping station. Day 140: 19 go north.
- [Proposed] Day ~143: the checkpoint levy (`levy-receipt`); Gary is beaten
  for arguing. All 19 reach Kell Bridge.
- [Proposed] Y+1: carves the grave marker for the Ridge Road drivers
  (`convoy-graves`).
- [Proposed] Y+3: Toby arrives at Kell Bridge; she recognises Kerry's boy and
  takes him in. Never receives Helen's letter.
- [Canon, 2026-10-10] Y+4: walkers bring rumours that Helen died at
  Northfield the winter before. Nobody can confirm it, and she keeps
  sending notes north.
- [Canon, 2026-10-10] Y+4: Dev is killed; Toby vanishes; she refuses to pay
  for water.
- [Canon, 2026-10-10] Y+4 to Y+5: Kerry's replies to Toby arrive at the
  exchange. She keeps them on the shelf behind the counter.

**Writer's truth** [Canon]: alive, 66, running the Kell Bridge exchange.
Grieving Dev, estranged from a daughter who is dead. She has heard the
rumour but has no reliable confirmation, and doesn't let herself believe
it [Canon, 2026-10-10].

**What a player can establish**: she ran limits and CS-4, kept 11 pallets
knowingly ("they had a truck, we had nothing coming"), gave away her seat,
left north with 18 others. Later records could add that the 19 paid a levy
and reached the checkpoint, and that she marked the convoy graves. Her
survival isn't stated by any planned record.

## Helen Lane

**Life before** [Canon in outline]: council emergency liaison. Ran the shelter
allocation lists and the school siren talks. Believed in procedure.

**Wanted**: an orderly evacuation, and her mother safe.

**Decisions and costs** [Proposed]
- 08:00 on the Ninth she broadcast that tap water was still safe. It was
  true then and never corrected.
- At 12:40 she held back the earlier plume forecast for over an hour to stop
  a rush on Bus 3 that could leave people on the road when the dust came, and
  phoned her mother first. CS-4 sealed in time; east-side households waiting
  outdoors for Bus 3 got 1 hour 45 minutes of warning instead of 2 hours 50.
- At Northfield she organised the Y+1 Ridge Road convoy, sending supplies to
  Kell Bridge that Northfield's council didn't want to spare. Its three
  drivers were killed by the Ash Hounds. She wasn't on it.

**Contradiction**: demands information discipline, and broke it for her
mother.

**Timeline**
- [Canon] The Ninth: liaison at the council emergency centre.
- [Canon] 10 March: on the radio from Northfield as "Liaison H. Lane", asking
  whether Ruth is in CS-4 (`radio-log`).
- [Proposed] ~14:00 on the Ninth: leaves for Northfield in the council
  liaison vehicle, after the siren and before the dust, to coordinate Calder's
  evacuees.
- [Proposed] Y0–Y+2: allocation work at the showground.
- [Proposed] Y+1: the Ridge Road convoy.
- [Proposed] Y+3 winter: dies of pneumonia at 41. Her last letter to Ruth
  goes with the trader Nell Ashby, who is killed on the Ridge Road
  (`helen-letter`).

**Writer's truth** [Canon]: died of illness, not violence. Never forgave
herself for the convoy or for 12:40.

**What a player can establish**: an H. Lane asked after Ruth Lane from
Northfield the day after the war; an "H." promised Bus 3; Ruth's book calls
her Helen. Later records could add her signature on the convoy manifest and
her unopened letter.

**Resolved contradiction (2026-10-10)**: an earlier proposal had Helen leave
Calder on Day 3. That clashes with the implemented `radio-log`, where she
speaks from Northfield on 10 March. She now leaves on the afternoon of the
Ninth, which also deepens her story: she warned her mother, then left.

## Dev Pillai

**Life before** [Canon in outline]: water technician at the pumping station:
pumps, cartridge servicing, the Loop cart routes. Writes short logs in
capitals.

**Wanted**: to keep water running for people, and to teach someone to do it
after him.

**Decisions and costs**
- [Canon] Kept a bore running and readings on one meter "on trust".
- [Proposed] Sent his one bore's water to CS-4 by truck, leaving the
  east-side households on the water main to their own Unit cartridges. He
  says it was because 140 people were in CS-4. Ruth was there too, and he
  never writes about that.
- [Canon, 2026-10-10] 18 May, Y+4: refused to show the Ash Hounds the bypass,
  the old scour line round the valve chamber that could keep water reaching
  Kell Bridge whoever holds the valve ([world-bible.md](world-bible.md#the-bypass)).
  They killed him, then chained the main valve.

**Contradiction**: trusts measurements, and runs on a meter he can't check.

**Timeline**
- [Canon] The Ninth onwards: the bore, the meter, the radio log.
- [Canon] Day 140: goes north with Ruth ("Dev says the old works main runs
  north…").
- [Proposed] Kell Bridge: rebuilds cartridge re-packing at the weir plant;
  returns each autumn to clear the old works intake.
- [Proposed] Y+3: takes Toby as an apprentice.
- [Proposed] 18 May, Y+4: killed by the Ash Hounds at the intake, aged 38.

**Writer's truth** [Canon]: dead. The valve he kept open is chained, and the
bypass he died for is still unopened and still unknown to them.

**What a player can establish**: D.P. kept the radio log, admitted he "sort
of knew" about the pallets, and had a bore and a meter. Later records could
add his toolbag and last log (`dev-toolbag`) and Toby's chalk account of his
death (`chained-valve`), which is a claim, not a witnessed fact for the player.

## Margit "Mags" Halloran

**Life before** [Canon in outline]: bike and mower workshop; fixed what Unit
inspectors failed, for eggs. A cat called Biscuit.

**Wanted**: to keep every Unit running, and to be left alone.

**Decisions and costs**
- [Canon] Declined her Bus 2 seat ("language"). The Patels left their Unit's
  keys with her.
- [Canon] The purifier stack in the player's Unit came from the Patels' Unit
  (U-118) by way of Mags. The serial number is provenance. Whether the Patels
  consented, whether the Unit counted as abandoned, and whether it was
  necessary stay **claims**. The player inherited this and did nothing wrong.
  No restitution quest in the first slice.
- [Proposed] The chain: on the Ninth she moved the U-118 stack into the
  Coopers' Unit (U-112), where a newborn was living on a failed seal. In Y+2
  the Coopers left for Kell Bridge and she moved into U-112, re-packing its
  own saturated stack for herself. In Y+3 she fitted the U-118 stack into the
  Ferrises' failing Unit, which is now the player's.
- [Proposed] The Patels' note says the keys were "to keep it ticking over";
  Mags's reply says "empty is empty, the baby needed it".

**Contradiction**: "I don't do people", and a job list of forty households.

**Timeline**
- [Canon] The Ninth: declines the bus; holds the Patels' keys.
- [Proposed] Y+1: the Patels walk back and find the stack gone.
- [Proposed] Y+2: leaves the workshop, which is too exposed; moves into U-112.
  Keeps a drop-off point at the workshop, visiting monthly.
- [Proposed] Y+3: fits the stack into the Ferrises' Unit.
- [Proposed] Y+4 to Y+5: leaves food for "the chalk kid" without knowing it's
  Toby. Refuses Ash Hound tribute; her door is tagged.

**Writer's truth** [Canon]: alive, 77, alone in U-112. The Ash Hounds want
her for her repair skills.

**What a player can establish**: she declined the bus and held the Patels'
keys. Later records could add her service tags on the player's purifier, her
recent drop-off notes, and the tag on her door. None proves she is alive; a
recent signature only shows someone signs "M.H.".

**Recent traces** (writer's causes): fresh pencil and oil at the workshop are
her monthly visits [Proposed]. The clean water bowl by the old office door is
kept full by a hose she rigged from the gutter [Proposed, from an earlier
note].

## Toby Wren

**Life before** [Canon]: Year 5. His mum Kerry works the tills; it's the two
of them. Loves the Loop carts, especially Cart 4, "Gerald", and secretly feeds
three depot dogs, Bigsy, Lady and Chips.

**Wanted**: then, for the dogs to be his and safe. Now [Proposed]: the valve
open (meaning the bypass Dev died for [Canon, 2026-10-10]), his mum's
answer, and to not be the kid from the drawings.

**Decisions and costs** [Proposed]
- At 14 he argued with Kerry and left Northfield for Kell Bridge.
- After the ambush he chose to stay in Calder rather than run for Kell
  Bridge: watching the Weighbridge, chalking warnings for travellers, keeping
  the dog pack close with a salvaged cart chime because the Ash Hounds avoid
  the dogs.

**Contradiction**: believed the dogs were his and would "be OK"; now keeps a
pack alive that is dangerous to everyone, him included.

**Timeline**
- [Canon] The Ninth: asleep in the staff room on Kerry's night shift; packed
  onto Bus 2 with a carrier bag of dog food; 46 of 48 arrived.
- [Proposed] Y0–Y+3: Northfield showground school; Kerry in the kitchens.
- [Proposed] Y+3: leaves with Kell-bound walkers. Ruth takes him in; Dev's
  apprentice.
- [Proposed] 18 May, Y+4: survives the ambush, escapes down the scour drain
  past the bypass gate to the depot underpass. He knows the way to the gate
  but only part of how to open it.
- [Proposed] Y+4 to Y+5: lives near the Nest. Writes to Kerry through the
  Workshop's forwarding tin; three letters go north.
- [Canon, 2026-10-10] The letters reached Kerry. Her replies went to Kell
  Bridge, care of Ruth, because nobody walks south into Calder. He has never
  had them, and thinks she hasn't answered.
- [Canon, 2026-10-10] **The player can restore contact** (the "Help Toby" task,
  [main-story.md](main-story.md#choices)): leave word for T in the
  forwarding tin; he answers and asks for his post, but doesn't want Ruth
  told where he is; word goes north with the Kell Bridge walkers who pay
  Mags in chits; Ruth sends Kerry's letters south to the payment tin with a
  note ("I won't ask where. Tell him the bench is his when he wants it.
  R."); Toby collects them and chalks his thanks at the bus shelter.

**Writer's truth** [Canon]: alive, about 16, hiding in Calder. Kerry is
alive at Northfield. A live character later is possible; nothing depends on
it.

**What a player can establish**: he was ticked onto Bus 2, and 46 of 48
arrived, which doesn't prove he did. Later records could add his Northfield
school card at the Nest camp, his chalk warnings and his account of Dev's
death, all signed "T" or "T.W.", which is likely but not proof of identity.
