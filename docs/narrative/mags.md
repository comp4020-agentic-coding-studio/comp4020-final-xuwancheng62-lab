## Who she is

**[Canon]** 72 on the Ninth (Tuesday 9 March, Y0), 77 now (autumn, Y+5).
She ran the bike and mower workshop that is now the Ruined Workshop, and fixed
what Unit inspectors failed, for eggs. She has a cat called Biscuit. She
declined her Bus 2 seat ("language") and held the Patels' keys. The purifier
stack in the player's Unit came from the Patels' Unit (U-118) by way of Mags.
She is alive, alone in U-112, and the Ash Hounds want her for her repair
skills. The player inherited the stack and did nothing wrong. There is no
restitution quest.

**Wanted** [Canon]: to keep every Unit running, and to be left alone.

**Contradiction** [Canon]: "I don't do people", and a job list of forty
households. [Proposed] A second one: she took the Patels' stack because
"empty is empty", and five years later their keys are still hanging where
they can collect them.

### Relationships, and how they change

- **The Patels** (Anjali, her husband, two children). Before the war,
  customers. On the Ninth, trust: the keys. In Y+1, a note and a hard answer.
  Now there's no contact; they are at Northfield. The keys still hang on her
  key board at the workshop. She has never said she was wrong, and never
  taken them down.
- **The Coopers**. A newborn she kept alive with someone else's stack. They
  left for Kell Bridge in Y+2 and left her their Unit. Gratitude at a
  distance.
- **The Ferrises**. An older couple she'd fixed for since before the
  Scheme. She fitted the stack, ate Mrs Ferris's quince paste, and heard Mr
  Ferris had died from a walker.
- **Ruth Lane** [Proposed detail]. Forty years of knowing each other at
  arm's length; Ruth bought her eggs for the staff room. Mags swore at her
  over the bus seat. Now they trade without meeting: Kell Bridge walkers pay
  for repairs with exchange chits stamped R.L., and Mags accepts them
  (`exchange-chit`, in Ruth's set).
- **Dev Pillai**, "Pump Boy". She's the closest thing he had to a teacher on
  motors, and the reason his rules come in quarters. After Day 140 she
  never saw him again. She heard he was dead in May, Y+4.
- **Toby Wren**, "the chalk kid". [Canon] She feeds him without knowing who
  he is. She knew him once as the Loop-cart kid on the east-side round, and
  doesn't connect the two. She forwards his letters without reading them.
- **The Ash Hounds**. Tribute refused; tools taken; door tagged. To them
  she's equipment. She isn't afraid of them, and that worries her more than
  anything else does.
- **Biscuit** [Proposed]. A ginger tabby, old now, with one torn ear. The
  reason she gave the council for not leaving. Alive, sleeps on the warm
  purifier housing.
